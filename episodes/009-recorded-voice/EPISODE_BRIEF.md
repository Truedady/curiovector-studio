## Scene arc (align to voiceover)
1. **0–5s:** a human hears phone playback and reacts; immediate familiar comedy and mystery.
2. Contrast one's own spoken voice versus recording.
3. First route: air conduction mouth → waves → eardrum → cochlea.
4. Second route: vibration through tissue/bone → inner ear; distinct warm orange visual.
5. Brain combines both routes into the usual perception. Show two waveforms mixing, illustrative not a literal exact spectral measurement.
6. Microphone capture mostly air pressure waves; bone vibration contribution absent in replay, with mic/speaker limitations.
7. Final perspective switch: how others hear the voice. `THIS IS WHAT THEY HEAR`.

## Science guardrails
- Both bone and air conduction influence self-voice perception; exact spectral effect varies by person/frequency.
- Do not claim microphones reveal an absolute objective voice; devices, acoustics and playback color sound.
- Do not show skull acting as an actual external resonant loudspeaker broadcasting perfectly; pathways are tissue/bone to inner ear.
- No bogus wave frequency labels or numerical percentages.
Reference: review peer-reviewed sources on bone conduction and voice perception if new claims are added.
