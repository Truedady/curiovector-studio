## Scene arc (follow MP3)

2. Reveal mic outside/inside earcup listening, processor and driver producing additional sound.
3. Demonstrate air pressure as wave, then phase inversion — two colored sine curves add and shrink near eardrum. Label `DESTRUCTIVE INTERFERENCE`.
4. Important: effect is local to ear, noise continues in cabin; show wave outside still present.
5. Need prediction/timing of anti-noise; steady hum easier to cancel, abrupt voices/clicks less reliably.
6. Seals/pads still give passive isolation. ANC isn't total silence.
7. Close: two colored waves meeting at the ear with `MORE SOUND. LESS NOISE.`.

## Guardrails
- Do not show perfect cancellation in real complex audio, especially with sudden broadband signals.
- Active noise control more effective for steady low-frequency sounds. Passive isolation remains important.
- Opposite phase is conceptual at the ear position; actual systems are dynamic, frequency dependent with feedback/feedforward designs.
- Earphone does not generate silence outside the listener's ear; source sound still physically exists.
