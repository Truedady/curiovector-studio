# CurioVector — Visual and Editorial Standard

## Identity

Premium illustrated science storytelling. The art direction established for the airplane, eye movement, butterfly and AI-memory concepts should remain recognizable across every episode.

### Core colors
- Deep navy `#08132F` — dominant background
- Electric cyan `#43C5F5` — light, motion and discoveries
- Warm orange `#FF923E` — emphasis, paradox, key callouts
- Off-white `#F8FAFF` — titles and readable diagrams

Use restrained gradients and subtle dimensional lighting. Avoid stock-image slideshows, generic AI photos, corporate PowerPoint cards, excessive neon, emoji-style illustrations and stock Remotion templates.

### Artwork
- Original hand-drawn/editorial-style SVG/2D illustrations with natural shapes, believable subject proportions and detailed lighting.
- One striking subject per opening frame.
- Elegant motion-graphics diagrams and clear labels, not constant bouncing text.
- Clear cinematic visual hierarchy, smooth easing, consistent spacing and typography.

### Thumbnails
- Cinematic painterly digital illustration, giant central subject, navy/cyan/orange contrast.
- Brief bold WHITE headline; selectively highlight one word in ORANGE.
- Strong visual paradox, minimal clutter.
- Landscape and Shorts thumbnails share identity but **must have separately designed compositions**.

### Voice and story
- Modern American English, clear and energetic narration, approximately two minutes unless directed otherwise.
- Hook within 3–5 seconds. No channel greeting or introductory logo.
- Curiosity → surprise → explanation → new discovery → satisfying reframing.
- A compelling new visual fact or perspective roughly every 10–20 seconds.
- Never use invented scientific facts or oversell correlations as proof.

### Formats
- Landscape 1920×1080 / Shorts 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- One audio master and common timing.
- Independent scene layouts for wide and vertical outputs.
- Especially avoid the earlier mistake of a small landscape subject suspended above a blank lower half of a vertical frame.
- Keep legible diagrams and safe areas for short-form platform overlays.

### Science and sourcing
- Separate direct evidence, illustrative demonstrations and hypotheses.
- Qualify uncertainty and study limitations.
- Record source links and verification notes in each episode brief.

**This guide is the single source of truth for new CurioVector episodes.**
